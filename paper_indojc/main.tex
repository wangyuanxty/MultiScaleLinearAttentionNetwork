\begin{jabstract}
    
Lithium-ion battery fleets---in electric vehicles, robotics,
portable medical devices, and second-life markets---generate a
continuous stream of maintenance and replacement decisions. Each such
decision requires models accurate enough to be decision-grade yet
small enough to run on the microcontrollers inside battery management
systems, forecasts that stay trustworthy as horizons lengthen, and
uncertainty statements that let operators trade risk against cost.

We propose DeltaCycle, a capacity-only monitoring loop built for
deployment. Its multi-scale linear-attention encoder is
deployment-verified via a single-branch variant that runs in C on a
microcontroller with a fixed-size recurrent state, matching PyTorch
within floating-point rounding; a physics-consistent degradation-rate
head driven by measured internal resistance constrains the decay
direction; and conformal-calibrated prediction intervals convert point
forecasts into decision-grade outputs.

Across five public datasets spanning four chemistries, DeltaCycle
achieves first-tier accuracy among protocol-matched baselines---state
of the art on PANASONIC. The physics term doubles unseen-tail
extrapolation fidelity and wins every corruption-robustness setting
tested. Interval coverage is lifted to $93.3\%$ at nominal $95\%$.
For battery operators this is a capability shift: continuous,
risk-aware asset monitoring on-device at near-zero marginal cost,
without cloud connectivity or model retraining.
\end{jabstract}

\section*{Data and Code Availability}

The datasets used in this study are public: CALCE (Center for
Advanced Life Cycle Engineering, request access), NASA (Prognostics
Center of Excellence Data Repository), MIT/Stanford
(data.matr.io/1), PANASONIC and TJU (as distributed with the
PatchFormer and RUL-Mamba repositories). Code for training,
evaluation, figure generation, and the MCU deployment is available
at \url{https://github.com/wangyuanxty/MultiScaleLinearAttentionNetwork}.
